Version 1.8
- Fixed: Unify Your Home Lands claims were not recognised when declaring war ("War without Casus Belli"). This is an old Imperator bug with claims given by script; the usual workaround is to queue a building and cancel it. The mission now does that refresh itself whenever it hands out claims. If you already have the claims from before this update, queue and cancel any building once and the claim will show up.
- New: faster auto-trade for players. Once a month, every state with automated trading switched on fills its free trade routes with goods it does not have yet: first from your own states, then from AI nations in the same or a neighbouring region. It keeps your capital's surplus for the capital bonus, never imports from other players and never from AI nations at war with you or that dislike you. AI nations keep the normal game behaviour.
