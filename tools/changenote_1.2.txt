Version 1.2
- New: One Province Nations. Every nation now starts with at most one province. Each nation keeps the province its capital is in, and every other province it held becomes a new independent nation (793 new nations, about 1,500 in total). New nations take their culture and religion from their pops and their government and tech from their old owner, and they are named after their capital.
- Removed: raised levies can no longer drill. Drilling works exactly as in Invictus again (legions only).
- New: Open granaries for allies (from HMO: Huge Multiplayer Overhaul). While you are at war, player armies of your allies, subjects and overlord standing in your provinces draw food from your province stores every month (unit size / 5 + 5 food, if the province has at least that much and at least 10 food).
- New: "Unify Your Home Lands" mission tree (requested by Nabber) for every independent nation that borders a territory where its primary culture is the majority. When the mission starts, every territory where your culture holds the majority becomes your homeland. Claim it all, own 25%, 50%, 75% and finally 90% of it, gain a kin nation as ally or subject, and grow a 20-pop capital. The final reward is a permanent "Homeland United" bonus.
- New (multiplayer balance, requested by Pope): no subjects at start. All 119 starting feudatories, satrapies, tributaries, client states, vassal tribes and temple states are independent.
- New (multiplayer balance, requested by Pope): no legions at start. The six Diadochi kingdoms (Antigonids, Seleucids, Ptolemies, Macedon, Thrace, Epirus) no longer start with a legion, and their military law is reset from "Work for the Best" to "Recruit from Nobles". No nation can recruit legions until it adopts a military law that allows them.
- Changed (multiplayer balance, requested by Pope): mission rewards that raised a free legion now give manpower instead, about 1,000 men per cohort the legion would have had: Parthian satrapy professional army +35, Diadochi Argyraspides +40, Hamilcar's Iberian army (Carthage) +20, Magas of Cyrene (Egypt) +7. If you already have a legion in your capital region, the existing reinforcement rewards still apply.
