Version 1.4
- New: levy experience, ported from HMO at x8's request. A levy records its experience when raised. Disbanding it with the new "Disband Levy" unit ability (or "Disband All Levies") gives military experience for the experience it gained since: 7.5% of the gain, max 10 per levy. Only levies led by a governor or the ruler pay out. The per-governorship vanilla disband button is removed.
- New: "Levy Veterans". While your military law does not allow legions, experience decay drops from 4.5% to about 0.5% a month, so levies keep their starting experience and are much stronger, but give less military experience when disbanded. Enacting a legion law removes it.
- Compatibility: the mod now replaces Invictus's gui/military_view.gui.
- Changed: "Unify Your Home Lands" now only covers your capital's region (reported by Shadout). Nations whose culture spread far, like Macedonian Bactria or Carthage, were asked to conquer every province of their culture from Greece to India or from Iberia to Libya. Your homeland is now the territories of your capital's region where the culture of your capital holds the majority. If your capital's people have nothing left to unite there, your primary culture is used instead. Overseas kin outside the region no longer count.
- Changed: "Bonds of Blood" (Unify Your Home Lands) now accepts any nation as ally or subject, not only one of your own culture, so every nation can finish the tree.
- Removed: the Invictus "Wars of the Diadochi" start event and its scripted war chain (Egypt, the Seleukids and Thrace declaring war on the Antigonids, Macedon's war choice, the ultimatums), along with the Legacy of Alexander claims it gave and the Diadochi province flipping tied to it. The mod now replaces Invictus's events/dhe/dhe_diadochi.txt.
