[h1]Molon Labe Invictus SubMod – Foreign Traditions[/h1]

A multiplayer balance submod for [b]Imperator: Invictus[/b], built for the Molon Labe community. It is based on the [b]Molon Labe Invictus SubMod by Pope[/b], which is included, so you don't need to run both.

[h2]Why this mod?[/h2]
In multiplayer, the 304 BC start decides too much. A few players begin with huge empires, ready-made legions and a ring of vassals, while everyone else spends the game catching up. Mature games tend to look the same: every nation stacks the same best foreign traditions, morale climbs with tech until wars are decided by who researched faster, and diplomatic shortcuts let the strong take land without fighting for it.

This mod tries to give every player a fair start and to make wars, alliances and expansion matter more.

[h2]What it aims for[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]An even start.[/b] Every nation begins independent, small and without legions. Empires have to be built during the game, not inherited from the setup.
[*][b]Identity over min-maxing.[/b] Your own culture's military traditions stay at full strength. Foreign ones are easy to study but much weaker, so borrowing from your neighbours is a flavour choice rather than the best build.
[*][b]Armies that are earned.[/b] Legions come from changing your military law, not from the start date or free mission rewards, and levies are worth raising and leading well.
[*][b]Real wars with lasting results.[/b] Land changes hands through actual wars rather than threats, and long truces make each peace deal count.
[*][b]A goal for everyone.[/b] Every nation, however small, gets a mission tree to unite the homeland of its people, alongside the historical Invictus missions.
[*][b]Keep the history.[/b] Invictus events, missions and characters stay. Only the parts that hand out subjects, legions or forced wars at the start are changed.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Requires [b]Imperator: Invictus[/b]. Load this mod [b]after[/b] Invictus.
[*]Start a [b]new game[/b]. The one-province start only works on a fresh game.
[*]Mods that change the 304 BC setup, the military and diplomacy windows, military tech or the Invictus events this mod replaces will conflict.
[*]With about 1,500 nations at start, the early game runs slower than a normal Invictus game.
[/list]
